Must-Visit Wonders with Shammi

From sacred heritage sites and coastal marine sanctuaries to thrilling wildlife safaris—select a destination below to add it straight to your booking.

Cultural Heritage

Aluthgama / Bentota

Kande Viharaya

Experience peace and spirituality at one of Sri Lanka's most revered Buddhist temples, home to the majestic 48-meter Samadhi Buddha statue, ancient Bo tree, and intricate murals.

Marine Conservation

Southern Coast

Turtle Hatchery Farm

Visit the famous Sea Turtle Conservation Farm (Kesba Farm). Learn how endangered sea turtles are protected, see adorable baby hatchlings up close, and witness ocean conservation in action.

Wildlife Safari

Deep South Wilderness

Yala National Park

Embark on an unforgettable 4x4 jungle safari in Yala—renowned worldwide for having one of the highest densities of wild leopards, alongside majestic elephants, sloth bears, and exotic birds.
